# Constella — AI video script (paste into your video app)

A recorded walkthrough, ~2.5 minutes, 7 scenes. For each scene:
**VOICEOVER** = paste into the AI narrator. **ON-SCREEN** = the caption/title
to show. **PICTURE** = what image to display behind it.

Tone for the AI voice: calm, confident, warm. Not salesy.

---

### Scene 1 — Title (8s)
**ON-SCREEN:** Constella — Every answer has a source.
**VOICEOVER:** "Every study app gives students more to read. Constella gives
them proof they understand."
**PICTURE:** The app's home screen, or a title card with the logo on the dark background.

### Scene 2 — The constellation (15s)
**ON-SCREEN:** Your courses, connected.
**VOICEOVER:** "A student uploads their own course material. Constella maps it
as a constellation — each course a cluster of stars, with glowing threads
connecting ideas across subjects. Because real understanding isn't memorising
facts, it's connecting them."
**PICTURE:** Screenshot of the constellation panel.

### Scene 3 — A card with proof (20s)
**ON-SCREEN:** Cite, don't guess.
**VOICEOVER:** "Every flashcard comes from their notes — and every answer carries
a citation to the exact line. Trust you can actually check. The app schedules
each card to return right before they'd forget it."
**PICTURE:** Screenshot of a flipped flashcard showing the gold source badge.

### Scene 4 — Ask, grounded (18s)
**ON-SCREEN:** Ask your own material.
**VOICEOVER:** "Ask a question — by typing or by voice — and Constella answers
only from the student's material, citing the line, and can even read it aloud."
**PICTURE:** Screenshot of the tutor panel with a cited answer.

### Scene 5 — The honest refusal (25s) ← THE KEY MOMENT
**ON-SCREEN:** It refuses to bluff.
**VOICEOVER:** "But ask something outside their material, and here's what makes
Constella different. It refuses. Honestly. No invented facts, no fake citations
— because a confident wrong answer is worse than an honest gap. Then it points
the student to trusted sources to add. This is the trust every other AI study
tool is missing."
**PICTURE:** Screenshot of the amber refusal + the suggested-sources list.

### Scene 6 — Any field, one engine (22s)
**ON-SCREEN:** Same engine. Any subject.
**VOICEOVER:** "Paste a completely different course — a cloud-computing outline
— and the same engine generates grounded cards, and threads the new subject
into the sky on its own, connecting cloud security to cybersecurity. A law
student and an engineer use the exact same app, with zero reconfiguration."
**PICTURE:** Screenshot of the constellation after a new course was added (new threads).

### Scene 7 — It knows where they stand + close (20s)
**ON-SCREEN:** Proof you understand.
**VOICEOVER:** "From their daily answers, Constella diagnoses — what's improving,
which topics they keep missing, and whether they're ready for the exam. Grounded
like Foundry IQ, connected like Fabric IQ, and trust delivered by design. One
mirror, for every student, in every field."
**PICTURE:** Screenshot of the "What to study next" readiness panel, then the logo.

## Full narration, as one block (if your tool wants it all at once)

Every study app gives students more to read. Constella gives them proof they
understand. A student uploads their own course material, and Constella maps it
as a constellation — each course a cluster of stars, with glowing threads
connecting ideas across subjects, because real understanding isn't memorising
facts, it's connecting them. Every flashcard comes from their notes, and every
answer carries a citation to the exact line — trust you can actually check.
Ask a question, by typing or by voice, and Constella answers only from the
student's material, and can read it aloud. But ask something outside their
material, and here's what makes Constella different: it refuses, honestly. No
invented facts, no fake citations, because a confident wrong answer is worse
than an honest gap — and then it points them to trusted sources to add. This is
the trust every other AI study tool is missing. Paste a completely different
course, and the same engine generates grounded cards and threads the new subject
into the sky on its own. A law student and an engineer use the exact same app,
with zero reconfiguration. And from their daily answers, Constella diagnoses what
they're learning, what they keep missing, and whether they're exam-ready.
Grounded like Foundry IQ, connected like Fabric IQ, trust delivered by design.
One mirror, for every student, in every field.
