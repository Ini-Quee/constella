## Full narration as one block (if your tool wants it all at once)

Students today aren't short on study material — they're drowning in it. Five
hundred pages a day, AI tools that invent facts, and the gap between recognising
an answer and producing it in the exam. Constella fixes all three. It starts by
understanding you: a quick survey captures your degree, your exam dates, and the
career you're working toward. Now the whole app is your plan. Erica studies Law
at Warwick, and she's also chasing an AWS cloud certification — so Constella
tracks both: her finals, her cert exam, the topics left to master, and exactly
how ready she is, updating from her answers every day. Her courses become a
constellation — Law and Cloud, connected — because understanding isn't
memorising facts in isolation, it's linking them across subjects. Every
flashcard is generated from her own material, and every answer cites the exact
line — trust she can check. And she doesn't have to sit down to study:
flashcards resurface as gentle notifications all day, so she learns in the gaps,
by voice or text. Ask something outside her material, and here's what makes
Constella different — it refuses, honestly, then points her to trusted sources
to add. This is the trust every other AI study tool is missing. Paste any course,
even a cloud outline, and the same engine threads it into her sky on its own —
one app, any field, zero reconfiguration. And from her daily answers, Constella
diagnoses what she's mastering, what she keeps missing, and what to study next.
Every study app gives students more to read. Constella gives them proof they
understand.
