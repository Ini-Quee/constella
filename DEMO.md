# Constella — demo runbook

You've got this. The app does the impressive part; you just narrate. Total
time: **3 minutes**. If you only remember one thing: **slow down on the
refusal (beat 4)** — that's the moment that wins.

## Before you start (2 minutes, once)
1. Use **Google Chrome** (the voice mic needs it).
2. `npm run dev`, open the URL.
3. Click **"Reset demo"** (top-right) so you start from a clean, known state.
4. Have one thing ready to paste later — an **AZ-900 / cloud outline** (sample
   at the bottom of this file). Keep it on your clipboard.
5. Take a breath. The judges want you to succeed.

## The one-line pitch (say this first)
> "Every study app gives students more to read. **Constella gives them proof
> they understand.** It studies only from *your* material — and it cites every
> answer or honestly refuses."

---

## The 6 beats

**1 — The constellation (10s).**
Gesture at the star-map drawing itself in.
> "Each course is a cluster of stars. The glowing threads are connections
> *across* subjects — because understanding *is* connection. No two students'
> skies look alike."

**2 — A flashcard with proof (20s).**
Press **space** to flip a card. Point at the **gold badge**, hover it.
> "Every answer carries a citation to the exact line in their own notes. Trust
> you can *check*." Press **3** to rate it Good — "and it schedules itself by
> memory, not by guilt."

**3 — Ask it something IN the material (25s).**
Click the suggested question **"When is an arrest lawful?"** (or click the
**mic** and say it).
> "It reads their material, grounds the answer, and cites the line — watch the
> ledger run." (Optional: hit **Read aloud** so it speaks.)

**4 — Ask it something OUTSIDE the material (30s — THE MOMENT, slow down).**
Click the second suggested question (the 2025 crypto one).
> "Now I'll ask something that's *not* in their syllabus."
It refuses in amber.
> "It won't bluff. A confident wrong answer is worse than an honest gap. **This
> is the feature every other AI study tool is missing.**"
Then point at the suggested sources that appear:
> "And it doesn't leave them stranded — it points to trusted open sources to add."

**5 — Same engine, a different world (30s).**
Open **"Add a course"**, paste the cloud outline, **Generate**.
> "Same engine — no reconfiguration." New cards appear, and **new threads
> animate into the constellation**, connecting cloud auth to cybersecurity auth.
> "It just found a connection between two different fields, on its own."

**6 — It knows where they stand (20s).**
Point at the **"What to study next"** panel.
> "From their daily answers it diagnoses — not a grade, a diagnosis: what's
> trending up, which topics they keep missing, whether they're exam-ready, and
> what to study next. The course they've mastered drops down the list."

**Close (10s).**
> "Grounded by Foundry IQ, connected like Fabric IQ, and the trust is delivered
> by design. One mirror that works for any student, in any field."

---

## If something goes wrong (don't panic)
- **Mic doesn't work?** Just type the question — same result. (Mic = Chrome only.)
- **Voice won't speak?** Skip it; it's a bonus, not the point.
- **Demo drifted?** Click **"Reset demo"** and carry on.
- **Notifications blocked?** Skip the bell; mention it as roadmap.
- Everything runs **offline** — no wifi needed.

## Sample cloud outline to paste (beat 5)
```
AZ-900 Azure Fundamentals
Module 1 — Cloud concepts
The cloud delivers computing services over the internet on a pay-as-you-go basis.
Module 2 — Network and connectivity
Virtual networks segment traffic; data moves across the network and must be secured in transit.
Module 3 — Identity, authentication and access
Microsoft Entra handles authentication of identities and access control to resources.
```
